About this stay

Welcome to Vistas de Gurabo, a peaceful countryside escape in Puerto Rico. Surrounded by breathtaking mountain views, this cozy retreat is perfect for solo travelers or pairs looking to relax and enjoy nature, whether you’re planning a romantic getaway, a peaceful workcation, or simply a break from the city. Unwind in the bathtub with scenic views, enjoy your morning coffee on the private balcony,. This thoughtfully designed space blends comfort, privacy, and the beauty of the outdoors.

The space

  • Cabin on top floor(third floor) fully equipped kitchen for all cooking needs( except condiments)
  • 12-inch memory foam mattress for extra comfort
  • Roku TV for all your streaming needs
  • Private balcony with expansive countryside and mountain views
  • Relaxing bathtub with a window view
  • Water tank and backup power generator for uninterrupted comfort and peace of mind
  • Air conditioning and fast, reliable Wi-Fi
  • One designated parking spot
  • Designed for up to 2 guests only

Other things to note

Parking for 1 car

Our home is located in the countryside of Puerto Rico, surrounded by nature and a true tropical island environment. Because of this, guests may occasionally encounter insects or small animals (such as ants, mosquitoes, lizards, or coquí frogs) even though the property is cleaned thoroughly and we perform regular professional pest control.

This is normal for the area and not considered a cleanliness issue. While we do our best to minimize encounters, it is impossible to guarantee a 100% insect-free environment in the tropics.

Lost or damaged items will be charged.
