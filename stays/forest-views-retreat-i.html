About this stay

Escape to Vista del Bosque Retreat, a peaceful studio surrounded by the forests and mountains of San Lorenzo. Enjoy fresh breezes, scenic views, and a quiet space perfect for couples or anyone needing a break from the city. The studio includes a private entrance, kitchenette, A/C, WiFi, and access to a shared pool overlooking the valley—close to restaurants, towns, and main highways while still feeling secluded.

Other things to note

Our home is located in the countryside of Puerto Rico, surrounded by nature and a true tropical island environment. Because of this, guests may occasionally encounter insects or small animals (such as ants, mosquitoes, lizards, or coquí frogs etc) even though the property is cleaned thoroughly and we perform regular professional pest control.

This is normal for the area and not considered a cleanliness issue. While we do our best to minimize encounters, it is impossible to guarantee a 100% insect-free environment in the tropics.

Pool Disclaimer

Please note that the pool is maintained and cleaned regularly from Monday through Friday. However, due to the property’s location on a hill, it is often exposed to windy conditions. Additionally, the pool is surrounded by trees, which may cause leaves and natural debris to occasionally fall into the water between scheduled cleanings.

This is a natural occurrence related to the environment and not a reflection of cleanliness or maintenance standards. We appreciate your understanding and recommend guests keep this in mind when booking.
