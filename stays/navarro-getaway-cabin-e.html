About this stay

Welcome to Navarro Cabin Getaway, a pure relaxation haven in rural Puerto Rico. Escape the routine, embrace nature's sounds and views. Perfect for serene getaways, this cozy cabin offers a unique blend of comfort and outdoor charm. Imagine unwinding by the pool (NO HEATER FOR POOL), or simply soaking in the tranquil ambiance. It's your ideal spot for a refreshing break, blending rustic appeal with essential amenities for a memorable stay.

Other things to note

Our home is located in the countryside of Puerto Rico, surrounded by nature and a true tropical island environment. Because of this, guests may occasionally encounter insects or small animals (such as ants, mosquitoes, lizards, or coquí frogs) even though the property is cleaned thoroughly and we perform regular professional pest control.

This is normal for the area and not considered a cleanliness issue. While we do our best to minimize encounters, it is impossible to guarantee a 100% insect-free environment in the tropics.

Pool Disclaimer

Please note that the pool is maintained and cleaned regularly. The property is often exposed to windy conditions. Additionally, the pool is surrounded by trees, which may cause leaves and natural debris to occasionally fall into the water between scheduled cleanings.

This is a natural occurrence related to the environment and not a reflection of cleanliness or maintenance standards. We appreciate your understanding and recommend guests keep this in mind when booking.

Lost or damaged items will be charged.
