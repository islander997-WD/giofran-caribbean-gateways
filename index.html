How we picked these

A listing with a 4.9 from 20 reviews has less evidence than a 4.8 from 116, so each listing's Airbnb star rating is pulled toward the average of all 25 listings (4.69) as if it had 25 extra average reviews. Listings with many reviews keep close to their own rating; newer ones need consistently high stars to rank. The top 6 by that weighted score are shown.
